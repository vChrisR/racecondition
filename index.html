The problem

Three systems that never meet

Your force-plate results, clinical history and event telemetry live in separate systems. So the readiness call you're accountable for gets made on gut feel and population averages — with no single defensible record behind it.

How it works

Real data → reasoned → your verdict

One clinical record

Performance tests, clinical history and event telemetry, unified on openEHR/EHRBase — the standard hospitals use.

Reason over it

The engine scores each athlete against the event's real demand profile, flags what's off, and drafts the plan to close the gap.

Your verdict, in the loop

Every flag gets a physician's label; those labels retrain a per-sport model, so it sharpens toward your clinical judgement.
